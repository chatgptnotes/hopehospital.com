Chest Pain - When to Reach Emergency

Thursday, 20 August 2026 - Cardiac Awareness
TO POST
Post Text
Chest discomfort should never be ignored. Warning signs that need emergency care: Chest pain, pressure or squeezing sensation Pain spreading to left arm, neck or jaw Shortness of breath with or without chest pain Cold sweat, nausea or lightheadedness If you or someone near you shows these signs, reach emergency care immediately. Hope Hospital, Nagpur provides 24/7 cardiac evaluation, ICU support and emergency cardiac care. Every minute counts in a heart attack. Location: Teka Naka, Kamptee Road, Nagpur.

Child Fever - When Parents Should Act

Friday, 21 August 2026 - Pediatric Awareness
TO POST
Post Text
Fever in children can be worrying. Most fevers are viral and resolve at home — but some signs need medical attention. See a doctor if the child has: Fever above 103°F (39.4°C) Fever lasting more than 3 days Difficulty breathing, rapid breathing or chest pain Seizure or extreme drowsiness Stiff neck or severe headache Refusal to drink fluids or signs of dehydration Hope Hospital, Nagpur provides pediatric consultation, emergency care and inpatient treatment when children need medical attention. Location: Teka Naka, Kamptee Road, Nagpur.

Kidney Stone Prevention

Sunday, 23 August 2026 - Preventive Health
TO POST
Post Text
Kidney stones can be extremely painful — but simple habits can reduce recurrence. Drink enough water: 8-10 glasses daily Limit salt and processed foods Eat calcium-rich foods but avoid excess supplements Stay active: regular exercise helps Don't ignore burning urination or blood in urine If you've had kidney stones before, regular follow-up with a nephrologist helps prevent recurrence. Hope Hospital, Nagpur provides stone treatment (RIRS, PCNL, ESWL), evaluation and preventive guidance. Kidney stones often run in families — prevention matters. Location: Teka Naka, Kamptee Road, Nagpur.

Dengue Warning Signs

Monday, 24 August 2026 - Monsoon Seasonal
TO POST
Post Text
As monsoon continues, dengue cases remain a concern in Nagpur. See a doctor immediately if fever is accompanied by: Severe abdominal pain or persistent vomiting Bleeding from gums or nose, or easy bruising Difficulty breathing or fatigue Cold, clammy skin or restlessness Warning signs usually appear after 3-7 days of fever — this is when urgent care is critical. Hope Hospital provides 24/7 emergency care, platelet monitoring, ICU support and hospital admission when dengue warning signs appear. Don't wait for complications — seek care early. Location: Teka Naka, Kamptee Road, Nagpur.
